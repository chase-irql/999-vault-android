# Known limitations and external blockers

- Physical Galaxy S26 Ultra evidence is not yet available because `adb` was not discoverable on `PATH` at initial inspection. Device-independent implementation continues while the local SDK is located or installed.
- Live OAuth depends on an owned deployed account service and user-present browser interaction. Deterministic staging coverage is required even when the live service is unavailable.
- Android Auto, Cast, Wear OS, widgets, picture-in-picture, public playlist sharing, custom cloud covers, Play Store rollout, desktop-file import, iOS, and a separately designed tablet product are intentionally deferred by scope.
